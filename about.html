Our Story

From Workshop to Trusted Partner

Al Hayen Technical Services LLC was founded with a simple belief: that industrial maintenance should be delivered with the same precision as the machines we service. Based in Sharjah's Industrial Area 2, we started as a specialized machine maintenance shop.

Over the years, our expertise expanded to encompass HVAC systems, MEP infrastructure, and property finishing — always maintaining the engineering rigor that our clients depend on. Today, we serve over 200 businesses across the UAE.

Our team of certified engineers and technicians brings decades of combined experience across industrial, commercial, and residential sectors. We don't just fix problems — we engineer lasting solutions.
